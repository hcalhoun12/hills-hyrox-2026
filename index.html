Race Stations
Women's Doubles · 8 × 1km run + station
Sub-1:30 Pacing Target
Running budget: ~44:00 total (8 × 1km @ ~5:30/km team pace). Station budget: ~30:00 total. Roxzone transitions: ~16:00 buffer. That's the shape of a 90-minute team race — adjust as real splits come in from training.
Rule that matters most: run at the pace the slower partner can hold for all 8km, not the pace the faster one wants to run at km 1. Losing 10s/km to run smart beats blowing up and losing 2 minutes at the next station.
